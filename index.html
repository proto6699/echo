About

I'm ech0. I build things in the messy overlap between software and hardware: UAV systems, Rust tooling, telemetry, Linux machines, embedded experiments, and custom airframes. The fun part is taking a rough idea far enough that reality gets a vote.

I also hold a GACA-issued Remote Pilot Certificate (Open category). This site is a selected slice of the den, not a complete inventory. Some experiments are still busy becoming respectable projects.
