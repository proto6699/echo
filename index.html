SYS.03
Hardware / Airframe

Echo 03

A sub-100 gram, 3-inch endurance quadcopter, built to see how far weight and drag reduction could push flight time on a single 18650 cell. Flight-tested to 27 minutes 12 seconds of continuous airtime at 87 g all-up weight.

87 G AUW27:12 ENDURANCESINGLE 186503" PLATFORM

Turns out "just make it lighter" eventually becomes an engineering methodology.

About
